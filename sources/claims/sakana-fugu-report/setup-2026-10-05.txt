4.1.1 Evaluation setup We tested the capabilities of the Sakana Fugu models on contemporary, challenging benchmarks unseen by our models during training to evaluate their generalization capabilities. For agentic coding and software engineering, we use SWE Bench Pro (Deng et al., 2025) and Terminal Bench 2.1 (Merrill et al., 2026). To best expose the underlying capabilities of Fugu, we use minimal evaluation harnesses such as Mini-SWE-agent (SWE-agent Contributors, 2026) and Terminus 2 (Laude Institute, 2026). To evaluate scientific knowledge, we used GPQA Diamond (Rein et al., 2024), the set of diamond difficulty questions on natural science taken from the Graduate-level Google-proof Q&A benchmark. For multidisciplinary reasoning, we used Humanity’s Last Exam (Phan et al., 2025), including multimodal samples, and evaluated without tools. To evaluate competitive programming, we used the latest full v6 release of LiveCodeBench (Jain et al., 2024) (problems taken from May 2023 to April 2025) and the latest publicly available and gradable split of LiveCodeBench Pro (Zheng et al., 2026) (problems taken from quarter 2 of 2025). For additional coding evaluation with scientific application, we used SciCode (Tian et al., 2024) with background provided. For multimodal graphical understanding, we used CharXiv Reasoning (Wang et al., 2024), and for conversational dialog, we used τ3\tau^{3} Banking with GPT-5.2 as the user simulator. Finally, for long context retrieval and reasoning, we used needle-in-a-haystack MRCRv2 (Vodrahalli et al., 2024) and long document information retrieval benchmark Long Context Reasoning (Team, 2025). See Appendix A for further details on benchmark evaluation configuration. Sakana Fugu models orchestrate a large pool of diverse models that includes state-of-the-art (SOTA) frontier LLMs such as Gemini-3.1-Pro (Google DeepMind, 2026), Claude-Opus-4.8 (Anthropic, 2026c), and GPT-5.5 (OpenAI, 2026d). Therefore, to assess Fugu’s ability to harness and amplify the skills of its individual workers, we evaluate Fugu against these same models, with the same maximum reasoning effort configured.
